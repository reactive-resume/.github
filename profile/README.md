# Reactive Resume

**An open-source home for your next opportunity.**

Welcome to the organization behind [Reactive Resume](https://rxresu.me), a free and open-source resume builder. This is where the project and its community come together to make creating a resume accessible to everyone.

We believe you should be able to tell your story without paying to download it, giving up your privacy, or losing control of your data. That belief guides what we build and how we build it: openly, with contributions from people around the world.

[Build your resume](https://rxresu.me) · [Explore the code](https://github.com/reactive-resume/app) · [Read the docs](https://docs.rxresu.me)

## Be part of it

You don't have to write code to contribute. Report a bug, improve the docs, translate a few strings, or help someone get started.

- **Contribute:** start with the [development guide](https://docs.rxresu.me/contributing/development) or [help with translations](https://crowdin.com/project/reactive-resume).
- **Connect:** ask questions in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/reactive-resume/app/discussions) or join us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/aSyA5ZSxpb).
- **Support:** help sustain the project through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/AmruthPillai) or [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/reactive-resume).

Thanks for being here, whether you're building your next resume or helping us build Reactive Resume.
